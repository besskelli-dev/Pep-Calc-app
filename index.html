Before You Use Any Number

  • Confirm vial strength matches the label in your hand.
  • Confirm your dose unit is correct: mcg vs mg.
  • Confirm your syringe type is U-100 before using units.
  • If unsure, stop and verify with a licensed clinician or pharmacist.

Sterile Handling Reminders

  • Wash hands and clean the vial top with an alcohol swab before each puncture.
  • Use a new sterile needle/syringe each time and avoid touching needle tips.
  • Do not reuse or share needles/syringes.
  • For subcutaneous site prep and injection technique, follow your licensed clinician's instructions and the product labeling.
  • Store mixed vials as directed by your pharmacist or product instructions.
